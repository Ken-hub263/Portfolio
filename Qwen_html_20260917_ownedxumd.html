02 / Philosophy

We believe that the boundary between architecture and product design is an illusion. Both require a deep understanding of material, light, and the human body.

Our studio focuses on creating timeless, sustainable environments and objects that age gracefully, reducing the need for replacement and honoring the resources used to create them.

Founded in 2018, the studio operates at the intersection of spatial design and industrial manufacturing, collaborating with artisans and engineers globally.
